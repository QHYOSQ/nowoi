CONTACT & CORRECTIONS

Turn a problem into an improvement.

An unclear explanation, an incorrect result, or a specific website-making question is worth hearing about.

Contact NOWOI

in@nowoi.com ↗

There is no live support desk or guaranteed response time. Use an email address where you can receive a reply.

Make a correction reproducible

  1. Include the page URL and the task you were trying to finish.
  2. Provide a small example input that is safe to share.
  3. Describe the expected result and what actually happened.
  4. Include your browser and device type if relevant.

Do not send real customer information, passwords, access tokens, or files you lack permission to share. A smaller example also makes a problem easier to diagnose.

Linking and reuse

Links to individual guides are welcome. Contact us about substantial republication, translation, or other permissions and describe the intended use. Third-party references remain subject to their respective owners’ rights.
