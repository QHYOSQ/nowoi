ABOUT THE GUIDE

A field guide for real projects.

The point is not how many pages we publish. It is whether a page helps someone finish a task.

Why NOWOI exists

A small website brings together content, code, design, and publishing. NOWOI puts those decisions into concrete situations: a table that needs to be shared, a form that needs better errors, or a release that deserves a careful check.

This guide is for independent developers, designers, and small content projects. Follow the route in order, or start with the problem in front of you.

How the content is made

This guide uses AI assistance to draft, organize, and check publicly available technical references. Examples are written for this project. Technical behavior is grounded in primary documentation from sources such as MDN, W3C/WAI, and web.dev, linked at the end of each guide.

Reproducible examples, limitations, and observable results are part of the articles. Editorial and automated checks have limits; they do not replace testing with your own project, data, and devices. Corrections are welcome with the input, expected result, and actual result.

What makes a useful guide

  • A specific person with a specific task.
  • Concrete input, steps, and an observable outcome.
  • Failure cases, trade-offs, and limits.
  • A clear distinction between facts, illustrative assumptions, and recommendations.

What is here, and what comes next

The first edition contains six bilingual walkthroughs and four working exercises. Maintenance starts with correcting errors, updating sources, and improving existing examples. There is no publishing quota or target article count.

This version embeds no advertising or visitor analytics scripts. If new services are introduced, the privacy explanation must change with them.
