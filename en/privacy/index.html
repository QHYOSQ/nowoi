PRIVACY, IN PLAIN LANGUAGE

Be precise about data.

Tool input, storage on your device, and hosting access logs are three different things.

What you enter in the workshop

CSV files, text, demo form values, and budget numbers are processed in the current browser. This version of the application does not upload those inputs to our servers and offers no cloud sync. Browser extensions, your device, and other services you deliberately open are outside that promise.

Saving on this device

The launch checklist does not save by default. Only selecting “Save on this device” writes checklist state to localStorage for this website origin. It does not store form values or CSV data. Disabling saving or using the delete/reset controls removes the associated record. Your browser can also clear site data. Other people using the same device may be able to see saved checklist progress.

Visits and third parties

This version embeds no advertising, analytics trackers, remote fonts, or third-party browser libraries. A hosting service still delivers the pages; hosting and network providers may process IP addresses, request paths, times, device information, and security logs under their own rules. Those records are separate from whether workshop input is uploaded.

Following an external reference takes you to that website, where its own privacy practices apply. Reading a guide here does not automatically request its external references.

Contact, changes, and scope

Contact in@nowoi.com. Messages you choose to send are processed by email services; do not include passwords, tokens, or sensitive data. This explanation reflects this version of the code. Advertising, analytics, cloud storage, or hosting changes require a fresh assessment and an updated explanation.
